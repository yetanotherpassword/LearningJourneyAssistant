# Learning plan for STU0067

Your results across the board sit in the low-to-mid 40s, so this plan is about steady, targeted rebuilding rather than any single crisis. The good news is that the gaps are clearly linked: object-oriented foundations from CSE1OOF feed directly into the algorithms and data structures work in CSE2ALG, so improving one will lift the other. We'll start with the persistent Data Structures and Algorithms gap and the isolated Object-Oriented Design and Implementation gap, then work outward to your capstone (CSE3CAP) competencies. Every score here is a formative signal to guide what you revise next, not a verdict on you.

## 1. Data Structures and Algorithms

**Evidence.** This is your lowest area and a persistent gap at 40.2% (CSE1OOF 42.6%, CSE2ALG 39.6%, stable across subjects). Your CSE2ALG:Central examination (38.0%) and CSE2ALG:Test (40.0%) were the weakest, with markers flagging implementing data structures and sorting/searching algorithms in Java and the overall objectives of the subject.

**Actions.** Re-read the marker feedback on CSE2ALG:Central examination and CSE2ALG:Test, then re-attempt the exam-style questions tied to CSE2ALG:SILO2 and CSE2ALG:SILO3 by hand-tracing and then coding a search and a sort in Java.
