# Learning plan for STU0075

You have a solid, consistent profile across your studies, with real strengths in your capstone work (Project Management, Industry Standards Application and Technical Communication all sitting near 78-80%). The two areas to focus on are foundational: Object-Oriented Design and Implementation (72.0%, flagged as an isolated gap) and Data Structures and Algorithms (73.4%, a persistent but stable gap across CSE1OOF and CSE2ALG). ## 1. Object-Oriented Design and Implementation

**Evidence.** This is your lowest area at 72.0% (CSE1OOF 72.2%) and is flagged as an isolated gap. Your CSE1OOF:Central examination (71.0%) and CSE1OOF:Test (71.0%) are your weakest OOF results, and markers repeatedly note the need for clearer reasoning, better technical precision and more complete testing or reflection.

**Actions.** Revisit the marker feedback on CSE1OOF:Central examination and CSE1OOF:Test, then re-attempt design questions tied to CSE1OOF:SILO1 by writing out object models that combine data and behaviour before coding. Redo a CSE1OOF:Practical demonstration style exercise for CSE1OOF:SILO4, implementing a small real-life problem end to end and adding a full test suite. For CSE1OOF:SILO3, refactor an earlier CSE1OOF:Assignment solution to reuse and share code, documenting your reasoning at each step.

Learning outcomes involved:
- `CSE1OOF:SILO1` -- analysis/design/implementation compared with object-oriented modelling using objects that combine data structure and behaviour
- `CSE1OOF:SILO3` -- code sharing and reuse through object-oriented techniques to reduce development time

Assessments to revisit: `CSE1OOF:Central examination`, `CSE1OOF:Test`, `CSE1OOF:Practical demonstration`, `CSE1OOF:Assignment`

## 2. Data Structures and Algorithms

**Evidence.** This is a persistent gap at 73.4%, stable across CSE1OOF (72.2%) and CSE2ALG (73.7%). Your CSE2ALG:Assignment (72.0%) is your weakest ALG result, while your CSE2ALG:Central examination (75.0%) was strong; markers want deeper justification, more consistent evaluation evidence and tighter links between decisions and implementation.

Assessments to revisit: `CSE2ALG:Assignment`, `CSE2ALG:Test`, `CSE2ALG:Central examination`
